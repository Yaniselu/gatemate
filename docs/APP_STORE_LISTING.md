## English

**Subtitle (max 30):** Boarding pass & airport maps

**Promotional text (max 170):** Every flight, gate and trip in one calm place. Scan your boarding pass, follow your plane live and find your gate with walking directions.

**Keywords (max 100):** flight,tracker,boarding pass,airport,gate,trip,travel,itinerary,layover,delay,map,group

**Description:**

Gatemate is your calm travel companion, from booking to baggage claim.

FLIGHTS AND BOARDING PASSES
- Scan the barcode on your boarding pass or import a screenshot.
- Keep all your passes in one place, with seat maps and your family’s tickets.
- Get reminders 20, 10 and 5 minutes before boarding.
- Follow your aircraft live when it is in the air, with altitude, speed and arrival estimate.
- See your flight on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

TRIPS
- Flights close together are grouped into one trip automatically.
- Plan with friends: shared trip, group chat, checklist and expenses, with settle-up in one tap.
- Invite people by link, e-mail or WhatsApp.

AIRPORTS
- Detailed airport maps with gates, shops, lounges and services.
- Walking directions to your gate and a "leave now" plan for the day of the flight.
- Airport rules: liquids, power banks, documents and customs.

AT YOUR DESTINATION
- City guides with top sights, weather, money, plugs and public holidays.
- Getting from the airport: ride-hailing or public transport, with an estimate for your group.

VELO, YOUR AI TRAVEL AGENT (18+)
- Ask anything about your trip, get a packing list or a briefing on your destination.
- Velo can make mistakes, so check important details with your airline.

AND MORE
- Passenger-rights information and a claim letter for delayed or cancelled flights.
- In-flight games that work offline.
- Your travel map and a shareable travel card.
- Dark mode, Face ID lock and 6 languages.

Gatemate is independent and not affiliated with any airline or airport. Always follow official airport displays and your airline’s instructions.
