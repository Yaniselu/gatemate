Gatemate support

Gatemate · Velosyntech · Craiova, Romania

Need help with Gatemate? Write to contact@velosyntech.com. We usually answer within two working days. You can also send feedback from the app: Profile → Send feedback.

I did not get the verification e-mail

Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. In the app you can ask for a new code. If you used Sign in with Apple and chose to hide your e-mail, our messages arrive through Apple’s private relay.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password, enter your e-mail and follow the code we send you.

My flight details look wrong

Flight times and gates come from public data sources and can be late or inaccurate. Always follow the airport displays and your airline. You can edit any flight in the app.

Gatemate Pro

Pro is billed by the App Store or Google Play. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. Tap Restore purchases in the app after changing phones.
