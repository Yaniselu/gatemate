Community Guidelines

Gatemate · Velosyntech · Last updated 27 October 2026

Gatemate chats are for friendly, helpful travel conversation. These rules keep everyone safe.

Be kind

No harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying or discrimination of any kind.

Keep it clean

No sexual content, graphic violence, illegal goods or dangerous activities.

Protect privacy

Never share other people’s personal information, booking references, passport details or payment data.

No spam or scams

No advertising, chain messages, fake offers or phishing links. Never send money to someone you met in a lounge.

Safety & security

Do not discuss security threats or attempts to bypass airport security. Report anything worrying to airport staff immediately.

Reporting & enforcement

Long-press any message to report it or block its author. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Violations lead to content removal, temporary restrictions or permanent bans, and may be reported to authorities.
