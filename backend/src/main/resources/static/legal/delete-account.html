Delete your Gatemate account

Gatemate · Velosyntech

From the app (immediate)

Open Gatemate → Profile → Security & privacy → Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email contact@velosyntech.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, trips, flights, boarding passes, family members, alerts, chat messages and device tokens. A trip you share with other people stays with them, without you. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Gatemate’s access to your Apple ID.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you, except records we are legally required to keep (for example payment records handled by Apple or Google), for the period the law requires.
